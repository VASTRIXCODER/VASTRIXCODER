Hey there. I build full-stack AI products, and I've been at it since 2021. I started with browser games in JavaScript. These days I ship apps that read real data, call real APIs, and have live demos you can click through.

## What I'm building

- **[Politicon](https://github.com/VASTRIXCODER/Politicon)** ([live](https://politicon.vercel.app))  
  An educational, non-partisan guide to bills in Congress. It reads official bill records and estimates how a bill could affect one household's budget, with its assumptions and uncertainty shown.  
  `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Claude API`

- **[LeadHunter](https://github.com/VASTRIXCODER/leadhunter)**  
  Finds local businesses that have no website, scores them by opportunity, and shows them in a filterable dashboard.  
  `Python` `FastAPI` `SQLite`

- **[GNRG Dialer](https://github.com/VASTRIXCODER/leland-dialer)**  
  A sales qualification desk where an AI agent walks a homeowner through their utility bill and books a consultation. It has demo, practice and live AI modes.  
  `Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind` `Claude API`

- **[Case studies](https://github.com/VASTRIXCODER/case-studies)**  
  Write-ups of builds whose code is private: a cinematic 3D homepage, a 3D-navigated CRM, and a browser-based sales dialer.  
  `React` `React Three Fiber` `Next.js` `Twilio`
